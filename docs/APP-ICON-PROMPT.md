# 0.1.25 应用图标

由内置 image_gen 生成，透明背景。设计保留刘海与三种额度状态的识别点，以暖白主图形、蓝紫陶瓷底色及薄荷绿、奶油黄、珊瑚粉构成。

正式源图：`Packaging/AppIcon-1024.png`。`scripts/make-icon.sh` 使用系统 sips / iconutil 生成各尺寸 AppIcon.icns，不增加运行时依赖。官网使用同一源图的带版本号副本。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final production macOS app icon for AetherNotchQuota, an AI quota companion with cute storybook animal sidebars. Create ONE exquisite, clear, friendly macOS application icon, front-on orthographic, square 1024x1024 canvas. Transparent outside a centered rounded-square squircle tile occupying about 88% of the canvas, generous correct macOS icon safety margins. Tile: softly luminous periwinkle and indigo satin ceramic, restrained lavender highlight at upper left, deeper blue violet at bottom, rounded corners and delicate bevel, sophisticated soft dimensionality. Central mark: a LARGE warm ivory simplified rounded screen or arch with a very clear centered notch cut into its top edge. Think a tiny friendly notched Mac display, not a letter, not a shopping bag or padlock. The ivory mark fills the central upper and middle area, with broad simple surfaces and a dark periwinkle inset that creates the notch silhouette. Under it, exactly THREE substantial short rounded quota-meter pillars, mint green, butter yellow, coral pink, gently increasing heights left to right, evenly spaced and visually integrated. Strong silhouette, balanced negative space, ultra-clean contours, recognisable at 32px. Warm cream material relates to a hand-painted storybook app family but use polished simple icon geometry, with no noisy watercolor grain, no intricate painting. Subtle ambient contact shadows and soft upper-left lighting, charming and tactile rather than corporate. No writing, no numbers, no letters, no vendor logos, no face, no extra symbols, no stars, no particles, no border frame around the entire canvas, no mockup context, no desktop screenshot, no watermark. The squircle itself is opaque; only its outside is truly transparent.
